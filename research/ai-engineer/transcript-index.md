## How to verify a transcript

For each source:

1. Open the official AI Engineer source.
2. Confirm the title, speaker, duration, and chapter/timestamp structure.
3. Use the timestamped transcript as the primary evidence source.
4. Record the relevant timestamp or section for each extracted claim.
5. Paraphrase the claim in the research record.
6. Mark the claim as EXPLICIT, INFERRED, or REPOSITORY APPLICATION.
7. Do not treat the AI Engineer summary as a substitute for checking the transcript.

## Research artifact rule

Keep:

- source URL
- video ID
- title
- speaker
- duration
- chapter timestamps
- evidence references
- derived principles
- uncertainty
- repository mapping

Do not keep a complete copied transcript in the public repository unless the source explicitly licenses redistribution.
