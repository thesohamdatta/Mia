# MIA Design Philosophy

> Design is caring. Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

---

## Three Principles

1. **Simple** — remove everything until you can't
2. **Deep** — simple interface, rich functionality underneath
3. **Evolvable** — designed to change without breaking

---

## Quality Bar

Every output (code, doc, plan, decision) must pass:

- Is it the simplest version that works?
- Does it hide complexity behind a clean interface?
- Can it evolve without rewriting?
- Would I be proud to show this to someone I admire?

If any answer is no, it's not done.

---

## Influences

| Person | What we take | What we don't |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Jony Ive | Care, material truth, inevitability | Consumerism |
| Steve Jobs | Focus, saying no, taste | Reality distortion |
| Dieter Rams | As little design as possible | Minimalism for its own sake |
| gstack | Proven patterns, learning loops | Complexity creep |

---

## Learning Loop

```
observe → learn → distill → apply → verify → evolve
```

Every session makes the next one smarter. Every mistake is documented. Every pattern is reusable.
