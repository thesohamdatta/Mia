# MIA Engineering Principles

> Canonical full source: [docs/core/principles.md](docs/core/principles.md)

MIA favours:

- safety and explicit human control
- small, deep modules
- simple interfaces with rich internals
- explicit contracts and ownership
- verification over confidence
- progressive disclosure and context discipline
- reversible, evidence-driven changes

For agent-specific engineering guidance, read [docs/core/agent-engineering.md](docs/core/agent-engineering.md).
